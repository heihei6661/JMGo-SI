# Code of Conduct

We want a technical, respectful and harassment-free community.

Expected behavior:
- Critique ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask clarifying questions.
- Respect privacy: do not post other people's data, logs or credentials.
- Keep discussions on-topic and constructive.

Unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Deliberately disruptive or bad-faith participation.

Maintainers may remove content or ban participants who violate these rules.
Report concerns privately to 2690325118@qq.com (see SECURITY.md).

This is a short form of the Contributor Covenant v2.1.
